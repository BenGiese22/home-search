# Six-House Tour Feedback

Raw calibration data from the six homes Ben and Megan toured in person before this
project started. One was a "yes" (Canossa Dr.), five were "no." The detailed
reasoning behind each verdict was never written down — this file is where it goes.

**Purpose:** this is what the scoring rubric (`src/scoring.py`'s keyword/year
weights, and eventually `src/vision.py`'s room-by-room photo rubric) gets checked
against. Two things matter most:

1. Where your actual reasoning **agrees** with what the rubric already scores
   (condition, outdoor/hosting, commute, sqft, parking) — confirms the weights are
   pointed the right direction.
2. Where your reasoning **doesn't map to anything the rubric currently scores at
   all** (school district, natural light, street noise, "just felt off," whatever) —
   these are gaps worth knowing about even if nothing gets built for them yet.

No need to fill every field precisely or to weigh your words carefully — free text
and gut reactions are exactly what's useful here. Skip anything that doesn't apply
or that you don't remember clearly. The 0–10 "feel score" fields are optional; only
fill them in if a rough number comes naturally, don't force it.

The one hypothetical we thought of was "If Megan is downstairs watching a movie, she has to go up this spiral staircase maze of 4 half floors to get to the kitchen." Overall, I still honestly like the house, just not as one cohesive unit.
